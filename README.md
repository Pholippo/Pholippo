## How I work

- **I own the "what" and the "why".** I decide what a product should do, and I write that down as hard rules every agent reads first.
- **Agents write the code.** I don't claim to be a programmer. My job is to direct the agents, review what they make and decide what ships.
- **Proof, not promises.** I test on real programs and real hardware. I ask for proof that a fix works and chase down whatever doesn't.
- **Real users.** Soul Codes has paying customers, and agentfenster is in beta testing. Feedback goes straight back into the next version.
